Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23) — one sentence, at most 240 characters, pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable.

## The entry

> FridgeCam livestreams the inside of your fridge to your phone so you can see what's in it without opening the door, for people who would rather watch their leftovers on a screen than trust their own eyes.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes that single sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. It is deterministic: the same bytes on every run.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
